Everything a small studio needs

A real team of agents

A coordinator (the mayor of your town) turns your objective into tasks with clear acceptance criteria, then recruits up to four agents at once — gameplay, graphics, audio, levels, QA… Agents reserve the files they edit, report problems to each other with proofs, and never approve their own work.

  • Tasks, dependencies and checks in one place
  • Team messages, open problems, restore points
  • Pick the models: economical, balanced or maximum quality

You stay in charge

When an agent makes a sprite, a 3D model, an interface (HUD, menus) or a sound, NOVARA checks it technically — size, transparency, floating parts, silent audio — then shows it to you. Nothing enters your game without your OK. Agents can also suggest ideas or ask you to choose between options.

Watch them work

Follow your agents as a town where each agent has a house, or as text with their live terminals. Bubbles say what each one is doing: writing a file, testing the game, thinking… On a break, they stroll, chat, play basketball or look at the sky.

Assets made on your computer

NOVARA installs and drives free creative tools for your agents — Blender, Inkscape, Krita, GIMP, LibreSprite, a local image generator (ComfyUI) — and its own sound synthesiser for sound effects, looping music and ambiences. Every download is checked against its publisher's fingerprint.

  • Pixel art kit, spritesheets and Tiled tilesets
  • 3D models in low-poly, PS1, toon or realistic styles
  • Visual effects: particles, shockwaves, holograms
  • An asset library shared between your projects

A library for all your projects

Everything you approve goes into the project's library — and, if you like, into a central library shared by all your projects. Agents look there first and reuse what exists instead of making it again. The Studio takes a request in plain words (“a 1024 wet cracked concrete texture”), picks the right tools and shows you the plan before anything costs a thing.

  • Versions, variants, favourites and restore
  • Local, hybrid or online tools — you choose

A workshop for sounds and effects

Describe a sound effect — a jump, a coin, a creaking door — and listen to several variants made on your computer. Compose looping music by mood, layer ambiences, process sounds, preview visual effects and shaders. Then send the one you like to your project.

Your AI accounts, your choice

Sign in to Claude Code, Codex (ChatGPT) or Gemini CLI from the app — NOVARA installs them for you. Pick your main AI; agents use your own subscription or tokens. Connect online services when you need them: Supabase, Cloudflare, Netlify, Roblox Open Cloud.

Test in one click — even on your phone

▶ Test starts your game. Scan a QR code to play it on your phone, follow your agents and approve assets from your sofa over your own Wi-Fi. The NOVARA Android app sends a notification when an agent needs you.

Talk to your agents

Dictate your messages: recognition runs on your computer, nothing is sent online. Choose silent dictation, written or voice conversation, or voice commands.

Plugins

A plugin adds themes, game templates to start new projects from, and guides your agents read. Plugins only contain files — never a program — and NOVARA checks them before installing. Making one takes a plugin.json and a folder.

And also

Signed updates

New versions are offered on the start screen, with what they change. The signature is checked before anything is installed.

English & French

The app follows your computer's language. Switch any time in Settings.

Resume anywhere

NOVARA remembers tasks, decisions and interrupted work. Restart the previous team in one click after a crash.

Four engines

Web 2D (Phaser), Web 3D (three.js), Roblox Studio with Rojo, and Unity — NOVARA starts test runs and reads the engine's output.

Interface sounds

Glass, wood, crystal: every theme has its own little sounds and ambience. All optional.

Private by design

Your projects stay on your computer. Your voice is recognised locally. Your phone talks to your computer over your own Wi-Fi.
